# Crypto Whale Dashboard

A real-time dashboard for tracking crypto whale activity, powered by [CryptoDataApi.com](https://cryptodataapi.com).

## What it does

- **Whale Radar** – see which coins whales are leaning long or short, sized by notional and ranked against their 30-day norm.
- **Whale Alerts** – a live feed of large whale buys, sells, transfers and liquidations.
- **Coin charts** – click any coin to open its price chart with big whale buys and sells plotted directly on the candles.
- **Market context** – market health, fear & greed, funding, open interest, volatility and liquidity regimes alongside the whale data.
- **Settings** – bring your own CryptoDataApi key (Free, Pro or Pro Plus) and tune thresholds, refresh rates and alerts.

## Data source

All data comes from the [CryptoDataApi](https://cryptodataapi.com/api) REST API and SSE event stream.
See the [API docs](https://cryptodataapi.com/api/docs) for endpoints and plan tiers.
